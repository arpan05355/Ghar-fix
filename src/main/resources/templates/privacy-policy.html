Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026
Terms of Service Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

GharFix ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can manage your information when using GharFix.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use GharFix, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Address
  • Location information, when permission is granted
  • Account information
  • Booking and service history
  • Information you provide when contacting customer support
  • Device and technical information required to operate and secure the platform

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process service bookings
  • Connect customers with service professionals
  • Provide customer support
  • Send booking and service-related notifications
  • Improve the GharFix platform
  • Detect and prevent fraud or misuse
  • Maintain platform security
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. Location Information

If you grant location permission, GharFix may use your location to help provide location-based services, such as identifying nearby service professionals or determining service availability.

You can disable location permissions through your device or browser settings.

5. Sharing Information

GharFix does not sell your personal information.

We may share necessary information with:

  • Service professionals involved in fulfilling your booking
  • Payment processing providers
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Analytics or technical service providers
  • Government or law-enforcement authorities when legally required

6. Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers.

GharFix does not intentionally store complete debit card or credit card details unless explicitly stated.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no online system can guarantee complete security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw certain permissions or consent
  • Request information about how your data is processed

10. Children's Privacy

GharFix is not intended for children under 18 years of age.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The GharFix website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for functionality, security, analytics, or improving the user experience.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be made available through GharFix.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact GharFix through the official contact details provided on the website.
